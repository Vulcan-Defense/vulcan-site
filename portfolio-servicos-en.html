Commercial assumptions

  • 12-month minimum agreement.
  • Monthly price includes platform support during business hours.
  • Promotional annual price equals 10 monthly payments, paid in advance.
  • An endpoint is a Windows/Linux workstation or server running Vulcan Agent.
  • Mobile is purchased separately and includes inventory, posture, telemetry and CyberShield integration.
  • Managed cloud includes 90-day standard retention, with 30 days in fast-search tier; initial sizing assumes 2–5 EPS per asset.
